As artificial intelligence continues to reshape industries, the conversation around worker displacement has never been more critical. California gubernatorial candidate Tom Steyer has put forth a groundbreaking proposal: a jobs guarantee for those affected by AI advancements. This initiative aims to provide a safety net for workers, ensuring they have access to new employment opportunities as their current roles become obsolete due to automation.

The implications of such a proposal extend beyond California. It raises important questions about how societies can adapt to rapid technological changes while protecting the workforce. With AI set to transform various sectors, a proactive approach to job security could serve as a model for other states and countries grappling with similar challenges. 

As we look to the future, how can we balance innovation with the need for stable employment? Steyer's proposal might just be the first step in a much-needed dialogue about the future of work in an AI-driven world.
