The launch of Phase 2 for "Neverness to Everness" marks a significant step forward for Hotta Studio, even as the game grapples with ongoing AI-related controversies. The latest update, which went live on May 13, introduces a new limited S-class character, Hotori, alongside exciting event content that runs until June 3. This bold move demonstrates the studio's commitment to enhancing player experience despite external challenges.

Hotori is featured on the Misty Tipsy Style Limited Board, a unique gacha mechanic that allows players to roll dice and collect rewards. With player-friendly features like carryover pity mechanics, the game aims to foster a more engaging and rewarding environment for its community. Additionally, the Bright Moon Special Arc Research Program introduces the S-rank arc Marching Beyond Time, further enriching the gameplay experience.

As we look ahead, the anticipation for upcoming characters Lacrimosa and Chaos adds to the excitement surrounding the game. With the backdrop of the AI controversy still fresh in players' minds, it will be interesting to see how Hotta Studio navigates these challenges while continuing to innovate. Will the community embrace these changes, or will the shadow of controversy linger over future updates?
