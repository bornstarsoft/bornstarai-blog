In an intriguing experiment, Stockholm's Andon Caf is exploring the potential of artificial intelligence in business management. While human baristas serve the coffee, an AI agent named "Mona"powered by Google's Geminioversees operations, from hiring to inventory management. This unique setup raises important questions about the role of AI in the workforce and the ethical implications of such technology in everyday business practices.

Despite the novelty, Mona's performance has been mixed, struggling to turn a profit in a competitive market. With over $5,700 in sales since its opening, the caf still faces challenges, including excessive inventory orders and operational missteps. These issues highlight the limitations of AI in understanding context and managing real-world complexities, prompting discussions about the future of AI in business.

As we witness AI's increasing integration into various sectors, the Andon Caf serves as a case study for the potential and pitfalls of autonomous business management. Will AI revolutionize the way we run businesses, or will it introduce more complications than solutions? Only time will tell.
